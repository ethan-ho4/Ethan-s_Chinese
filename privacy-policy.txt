Privacy Policy for Ethan’s Chinese

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Ethan’s Chinese (“the App”) is a Mandarin learning app. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

Contact
If you have questions about this policy, contact: ethan.wm.ho@gmail.com

Information the App Stores
The App stores learning progress and preferences on your device only, including:
- Vocabulary words you have unlocked
- Practice preferences (such as script choice and topic selections)

This information stays on your device using on-device storage. We do not create user accounts, and we do not upload your learning progress to our servers.

Information We Do Not Collect
The App does not collect:
- Name, email, phone number, or other account identifiers
- Location data
- Photos, camera input, or microphone recordings
- Advertising identifiers
- Analytics or tracking data for advertising

Third Parties
The App does not sell personal information and does not share learning progress with third-party analytics or advertising services.

Children’s Privacy
The App is intended for general language learning use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

How to Use This Document
1. Paste this text into a Google Doc (or host it as a public webpage).
2. Make the document viewable by anyone with the link (or publish to the web).
3. Copy that public URL into App Store Connect → App Information → Privacy Policy URL.
4. In App Privacy, do not declare Camera, Photos, or Microphone. For this app’s on-device-only progress, “Data Not Collected” is typically accurate unless you later add cloud/analytics features.
